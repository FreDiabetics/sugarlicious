## AndroidAPS compatibility and historical provenance

The retired classic/community Watch Face Format ports are no longer shipped
or built. Their historical source and license records remain under `LICENSES/`
so prior repository revisions and release artifacts retain their provenance.

The names AndroidAPS and AAPS identify compatibility and source provenance.
This independent project is not an official AndroidAPS release.

## Sugarlicious and FreDiabetics branding

The Sugarlicious product identity and both Sugarlicious watchface layouts are
original project work. The FreDiabetics logo was supplied directly by the
project owner on 2026-08-06; its source hash and use are recorded in
`LICENSES/FreDiabetics-Logo.md`. No Apple artwork, hand asset or font is
included in the Sugarlicious Analog watchface.
